How it works

One hotkey, three steps.

1

Press to talk

Hold Ctrl + Option. The menu bar icon glows — the AI is listening. Speak naturally, describe what you need.

2

AI sees & understands

The AI processes your voice, reads your screen via streaming screenshots, and reasons about what you're looking at.

3

AI responds & acts

The AI replies in voice, highlights elements on your screen, and can execute multi-step action plans on your behalf.
